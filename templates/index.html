How it works

  1. Upload a JPG or PNG image of a rice grain.
  2. The server normalizes it to JPG, resizes it and runs the CNN.
  3. You get the predicted variety and top probabilities.

Model

Architecture: MobileNetV2 (ImageNet weights) with a fine-tuned classification head and on-the-fly data augmentation.
Served by Flask; the model is loaded once at server start.
